# 0002: MVVM with @Observable ViewModels

**Status:** Accepted

## Context
Views must stay free of business logic and side effects, and the UI must be testable
without rendering. Swift Concurrency and Observation are available.

## Decision
Each significant screen has a View, a ViewModel and Models. ViewModels are
`@MainActor @Observable final class`es. They expose `private(set)` state and methods, never
import SwiftUI, and depend on services and protocols injected through their initializer.
Business rules live in services or pure helpers, which ViewModels orchestrate.

## Alternatives
- **The Composable Architecture (TCA)**: strong guarantees, but a heavy dependency and
  learning curve. It is disproportionate for the team size, and it conflicts with "no internal
  frameworks".
- **Views with `@State` + services directly**: less code, but the logic becomes untestable and
  views grow large.
- **`ObservableObject` + `@Published`**: superseded by Observation, and it causes coarser view
  invalidation.

## Consequences
- ViewModels are tested with plain Swift Testing (`@MainActor` suites).
- There is a risk of large ViewModels. It is mitigated by extracting pure types
  (`TranscriptReducer`, `FuzzyMatcher`) and by a coordinator (`WorkspaceViewModel`) that holds
  no business rules.
